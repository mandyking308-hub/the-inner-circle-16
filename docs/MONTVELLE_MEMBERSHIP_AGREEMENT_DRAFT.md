# Montvelle Membership Agreement — counsel review draft

**NOT FOR SIGNATURE — COUNSEL REVIEW REQUIRED**

This draft is designed to sit behind the accepted-member payment page and be converted into the final agreement once legal review is complete.

## Parties

This Membership Agreement (the **Agreement**) is between **Global Solutions Management LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company (**GSM**, **Montvelle**, **we**, **us**) and the individual, family office, family entity or other approved applicant identified in the Membership Schedule (**Member**, **you**).

The Membership Schedule forms part of this Agreement and records the approved membership holder, authorised family users, term, fees, billing currency, start date and any bespoke service terms.

## 1. Membership

1.1 Montvelle is a private, invitation-only membership community and private-service platform.

1.2 Membership may include access to peer Tables, gatherings, member introductions, editorial and knowledge resources, global-life coordination, concierge/private-office coordination, family learning, member technology and other services made available to the applicable membership level.

1.3 Membership is personal to the approved Member and authorised users named or permitted under the Membership Schedule. It may not be sold, transferred, shared or sublicensed without GSM's prior written consent.

1.4 GSM may conduct identity, background, sanctions, fraud, safety or other checks reasonably required to protect the community, comply with law or assess continued membership suitability.

1.5 Membership begins only when GSM has accepted the Member, the Agreement and Membership Schedule have been validly accepted, any required checks are complete, and all amounts due at commencement have cleared.

## 2. Term and renewal

2.1 Unless the Membership Schedule states otherwise, membership runs for 12 months from the membership start date.

2.2 Membership does not automatically renew unless the Membership Schedule expressly says that it does. GSM may invite the Member to renew before expiry and may change the renewal price, service scope or other commercial terms for a future membership term.

2.3 A renewal becomes binding only when the renewal terms are accepted and the applicable renewal fee is paid.

## 3. Fees and payment

3.1 The Member must pay the annual membership fee and any one-time admission/onboarding fee shown in the Membership Schedule.

3.2 Fees are payable annually in advance unless GSM expressly agrees otherwise in writing.

3.3 Third-party goods and services — including travel, accommodation, event tickets, transport, property, healthcare, education, professional advice and other externally supplied items — are separate from the membership fee unless the Membership Schedule expressly states otherwise.

3.4 Taxes, duties, levies, bank charges and similar amounts are payable as stated on the applicable invoice and as required by law.

3.5 Where payment is made in a currency other than the currency shown in the Membership Schedule, the amount payable must be separately agreed. GSM is not required to use a live retail FX rate.

3.6 GSM may suspend access for overdue undisputed amounts after reasonable notice, subject to mandatory law.

3.7 Refunds, cancellation rights and cooling-off rights are governed by the Membership Schedule, this Agreement and any mandatory rights that apply to the Member. Nothing in this Agreement removes a right that cannot lawfully be waived.

## 4. Private service and third-party providers

4.1 Montvelle may research, source, coordinate or introduce third-party providers requested by the Member.

4.2 Unless GSM expressly agrees in writing to be the supplier, the third party — not GSM — supplies the relevant goods, advice or services and is responsible for its own acts, omissions, licences, professional duties, service terms and outcomes.

4.3 The Member is responsible for deciding whether to instruct or transact with a third party and for conducting any due diligence appropriate to the Member's circumstances.

4.4 GSM does not guarantee availability, reservations, upgrades, access, school places, visas, transactions, introductions, investment opportunities, medical outcomes, professional outcomes or any other third-party result.

4.5 GSM may receive commissions, referral fees, rebates, benefits or preferential commercial terms from third parties only in accordance with the disclosure and conflicts standard approved for Montvelle. Material arrangements that could reasonably affect the Member's decision should be disclosed where required by law or Montvelle policy.

## 5. No regulated or professional advice by Montvelle

5.1 Montvelle is a private membership, community, coordination and lifestyle service. GSM is not acting as a law firm, tax adviser, investment adviser, broker-dealer, immigration adviser, medical provider, fiduciary, trustee or other regulated professional merely by providing membership, information, introductions or coordination.

5.2 General materials, community discussions, peer perspectives, software outputs and operational suggestions are not a substitute for advice from appropriately qualified professionals instructed for the Member's own circumstances.

5.3 Where a matter requires regulated or licensed advice, the Member must obtain that advice from an appropriately qualified professional.

## 6. Member conduct

6.1 The Member and authorised users must act lawfully, respectfully and in a manner consistent with the private nature of Montvelle.

6.2 The following are prohibited unless expressly authorised: scraping; bulk export; list building; credential sharing; impersonation; doxxing; unauthorised recording; unauthorised screenshots or forwarding; unsolicited commercial prospecting; recruitment poaching; harassment; circumvention of access controls; misuse of confidential information; and use of Montvelle data to train an external model or dataset.

6.3 The Member must not represent that GSM, Montvelle or another member endorses the Member, an investment, a transaction, a product or a third-party provider unless expressly authorised in writing.

## 7. Confidentiality and introductions

7.1 Information shared in a private Table, Decision Room, member-only area, private event, concierge request, family context, ownership context, adviser context or confidential communication must be treated as confidential where a reasonable person would understand it to be private.

7.2 Members may use general learning and ideas from the community but must not identify another person or disclose that person's confidential facts, business information, family information or commercially sensitive circumstances without permission.

7.3 Private sessions may not be recorded, transcribed, photographed, screenshotted, forwarded or published without appropriate consent.

7.4 Montvelle introductions are consent-led. GSM may share enough context to establish relevance but should not release private contact details or sensitive case information to another member or Trusted Partner until appropriate consent is obtained.

7.5 Confidentiality obligations survive the end of membership. Nothing prevents disclosure required by law, court order or competent authority, provided the disclosing party follows any lawful notification restriction and, where legally permitted, gives reasonable notice.

## 8. Family and under-18 participants

8.1 A person under 18 may not enter into this Agreement directly with GSM.

8.2 A parent, guardian or other legally authorised adult must approve any under-18 participation and is responsible for ensuring that information provided about the child is lawfully shared.

8.3 GSM may apply separate age, supervision, account-access, communications, safeguarding and event rules to next-generation programmes.

8.4 GSM may immediately restrict participation where it reasonably believes safety, welfare, safeguarding or legal compliance is at risk.

## 9. Member information and privacy

9.1 GSM processes personal information in accordance with the Montvelle Privacy Notice and applicable law.

9.2 Member-directory information is private by default. GSM does not sell the member directory or grant third parties unrestricted prospecting access to member identities.

9.3 The Member grants GSM a limited right to host, process, reproduce and transmit information the Member submits only to the extent reasonably required to provide, secure and administer the service, comply with law and exercise GSM's rights under this Agreement.

9.4 Ownership of the Member's own confidential information remains with the Member or its lawful owner. Nothing in this Agreement transfers ownership of that information to GSM.

9.5 GSM may use software and AI-assisted tools to organise information, support research, summarise material, coordinate workflows or surface options. Material membership, safety or similarly significant decisions should include human review where appropriate or legally required.

9.6 Confidential member content should not be used to train a public or general-purpose model without an appropriate legal basis and any consent required by law or Montvelle policy.

## 10. Intellectual property

10.1 GSM or its licensors own the Montvelle name, marks, visual identity, website, software, private frameworks, editorial content and original materials, except for material expressly identified as belonging to a member or third party.

10.2 During active membership, GSM grants the Member a limited, personal, revocable, non-exclusive, non-transferable right to access and use Montvelle materials for the Member's own private purposes.

10.3 The Member may not reproduce, resell, republish, reverse engineer, commercially exploit, remove proprietary notices from, or create confusingly similar products from Montvelle materials except to the extent a non-waivable legal right permits it.

## 11. Availability and changes

11.1 GSM aims to provide a reliable and secure service but does not guarantee uninterrupted or error-free access.

11.2 GSM may change features, suppliers, programme formats, locations, digital functionality or operating processes where reasonably necessary for security, legal compliance, service quality, capacity or product development.

11.3 GSM will not use a routine product update to remove a material paid contractual right during a current term without following the applicable Agreement, Membership Schedule and mandatory law.

## 12. Compliance, sanctions and prohibited activity

12.1 GSM may refuse, delay, restrict or terminate a membership, request, payment or introduction where GSM reasonably believes continuing could breach applicable sanctions, export controls, anti-bribery law, fraud controls, financial-crime obligations, court orders or other law.

12.2 The Member must not use Montvelle to facilitate illegal payments, bribery, corruption, money laundering, sanctions evasion, fraud, trafficking, prohibited investment activity or other unlawful conduct.

12.3 GSM may request information reasonably required to assess a compliance concern and may make disclosures to competent authorities where legally required.

## 13. Suspension and termination

13.1 GSM may suspend access while investigating a credible security, safety, confidentiality, payment, legal or serious conduct concern.

13.2 GSM may terminate membership for material breach; repeated breach; serious confidentiality or no-solicitation violation; fraud; unlawful activity; material non-payment; serious safety risk; sanctions/compliance concerns; or conduct that materially threatens the trust, privacy or integrity of the community.

13.3 Where appropriate and lawful, significant membership decisions should include human review.

13.4 The financial consequences of termination, including any refund or retained fee, are governed by the Membership Schedule, the circumstances of termination and mandatory law.

13.5 Provisions intended by their nature to survive termination — including confidentiality, IP, accrued payment obligations, liability, dispute provisions and data-related obligations — continue after membership ends.

## 14. Member responsibility

14.1 The Member remains responsible for personal, family, commercial, investment, tax, legal, immigration, medical, educational, travel and other decisions.

14.2 Information obtained through Montvelle may be incomplete, become outdated or reflect another member's personal experience rather than professional advice.

14.3 The Member is responsible for ensuring that any person authorised to use the membership complies with this Agreement.

## 15. Liability

**Counsel to finalise this section for enforceability across target jurisdictions.**

15.1 Nothing in this Agreement excludes or limits liability that cannot lawfully be excluded or limited, including liability for fraud or fraudulent misrepresentation where applicable.

15.2 Subject to clause 15.1, GSM should not be liable for indirect, incidental, consequential, special or punitive loss, or for loss of profit, revenue, business opportunity, goodwill, anticipated savings or investment value, to the fullest extent permitted by applicable law.

15.3 Subject to clause 15.1, GSM should not be liable for the acts, omissions, insolvency, delay, negligence, cancellation or non-performance of an independent third-party supplier except to the extent applicable law imposes non-excludable responsibility on GSM.

15.4 **Counsel to insert an aggregate liability cap appropriate to the commercial model and applicable law.** A commonly considered structure is a cap linked to fees paid by the Member to GSM during the relevant 12-month period, with separate treatment for liabilities that cannot lawfully be capped.

## 16. Indemnity

**Counsel to confirm whether and how this clause should apply to consumer, individual and entity memberships.**

Where lawful and appropriate, an entity Member should indemnify GSM against third-party claims arising directly from the entity's unlawful use of Montvelle, infringement caused by material supplied by the entity, or unauthorised acts of its authorised users, excluding losses caused by GSM's own non-excludable fault.

## 17. Events outside reasonable control

Neither party is responsible for delay or failure caused by events outside its reasonable control to the extent permitted by law, including natural disasters, war, terrorism, civil disruption, epidemic/pandemic restrictions, government action, transport disruption, widespread communications outages, supplier failures or similar force-majeure events. Mandatory refund or consumer rights remain unaffected.

## 18. Electronic contracting and notices

18.1 This Agreement may be accepted electronically and in counterparts.

18.2 The Member agrees that notices relating to routine membership administration may be sent electronically to the contact details recorded in the membership account or Membership Schedule.

18.3 Formal legal notices must be sent using the notice details stated in the Membership Schedule or Legal Notice.

## 19. Assignment

19.1 The Member may not assign or transfer this Agreement or membership without GSM's prior written consent.

19.2 GSM may assign this Agreement to an affiliate, successor or purchaser of substantially all of the relevant business or assets, subject to applicable law and without reducing the Member's mandatory rights.

## 20. Entire agreement and order of precedence

20.1 The Agreement, Membership Schedule, incorporated privacy/confidentiality documents and any expressly incorporated service schedule form the entire agreement for the paid membership relationship and supersede prior statements about that relationship.

20.2 If documents conflict, the order of precedence should be: (1) signed Membership Schedule / bespoke service schedule; (2) this Membership Agreement; (3) incorporated policies; (4) public website terms, unless the relevant document expressly states otherwise.

## 21. Changes

21.1 GSM may update policies and operational rules for legitimate security, legal or service reasons.

21.2 A material change to a current paid contractual right should not bind the Member during the current term without the notice, consent or other process required by the Agreement or applicable law.

## 22. No waiver; severability

A failure to enforce a provision is not a waiver of that provision. If a provision is held invalid or unenforceable, it should be modified or severed to the minimum extent necessary and the remaining provisions continue in effect, subject to applicable law.

## 23. Relationship of the parties

Nothing in this Agreement creates a partnership, joint venture, employment relationship, fiduciary relationship, agency or authority for one party to bind the other, except where a separate written document expressly says otherwise.

## 24. Governing law and disputes

**Counsel decision required.**

Proposed starting point: the Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, United States, without prejudice to mandatory rights that apply because of the Member's place of residence or other applicable law.

Counsel should select and draft the dispute forum — Delaware courts or confidential arbitration — and ensure the clause is suitable for individual/consumer as well as entity memberships in target markets.

## 25. Language

Translations may be provided for convenience. The English-language Agreement is authoritative to the extent permitted by applicable law.
