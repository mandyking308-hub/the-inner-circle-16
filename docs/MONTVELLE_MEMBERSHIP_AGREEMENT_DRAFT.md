# Montvelle Membership Agreement — counsel review draft

**NOT FOR SIGNATURE — GARY / COUNSEL REVIEW REQUIRED BEFORE LIVE MEMBERSHIP SALES**

This draft is intended to sit behind the public Montvelle membership purchase flow and be finalised before Dodo Payments is switched to live mode.

## Parties and structure

This Membership Agreement (the **Agreement**) is between **Global Solutions Management LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company (**GSM**, **Montvelle**, **we**, **us**) and the individual, family entity, family office, company or other approved purchaser identified in the applicable Membership Schedule (**Member**, **you**).

The Membership Schedule forms part of this Agreement and records the member legal name, authorised users, contracting capacity, term, price, billing currency, start date and any bespoke terms. If documents conflict, the order of precedence is: (1) Membership Schedule / bespoke service schedule; (2) this Agreement; (3) incorporated Privacy, Confidentiality and conduct policies; (4) public Website Terms, unless a document expressly says otherwise.

## 1. Membership and admission

1.1 Montvelle is a private global membership community, coordination and private-service platform.

1.2 Membership may include peer relationships and Tables, private gatherings, introductions, knowledge resources, Global Life coordination, Concierge / Private Office coordination, family learning, member technology and other services made available to the applicable membership relationship.

1.3 The ability to submit payment through the public website does not compel GSM to admit a purchaser. GSM may conduct identity, sanctions, fraud, safety, suitability and other checks reasonably required to protect the community or comply with law.

1.4 Membership becomes active only when GSM has accepted the purchaser, this Agreement and the Membership Schedule have been validly accepted, required checks have been completed and all commencement amounts have cleared.

1.5 If GSM declines a purchaser before membership activation after payment has been taken, GSM will arrange return of the membership fee and admission/onboarding fee paid for the unactivated membership, subject to mandatory law and the payment-provider reversal process. **Counsel to confirm the precise contract-formation analysis and refund wording for target jurisdictions.**

1.6 Membership is personal to the approved Member and authorised users stated or permitted in the Membership Schedule. It may not be sold, transferred, shared, sublicensed or used to provide access to another person without GSM's prior written consent.

## 2. Term, renewal and future pricing

2.1 Unless the Membership Schedule states otherwise, membership runs for 12 months from the agreed activation date.

2.2 At launch, membership does **not** automatically renew. Before expiry GSM may invite the Member to renew at the then-current fee and on the then-current renewal terms.

2.3 GSM may change prices for future new memberships and renewals. A previous or publicly displayed fee does not create a right to a future or grandfathered price. The price for a renewal is the price stated in the applicable renewal invitation / Membership Schedule.

2.4 A renewal becomes binding only when the Member accepts the renewal terms and the renewal fee is paid, unless a future Membership Schedule expressly adopts an automatic-renewal mechanism that complies with applicable law.

2.5 **Counsel to approve the minimum renewal-price notice period and whether any territory requires additional renewal notices or express consent.**

## 3. Fees and payment

3.1 The Member must pay the annual membership fee and any admission/onboarding fee shown in the Membership Schedule or accepted checkout.

3.2 The 2026 standard founding position proposed for counsel approval is:
- annual membership: **£35,000** for 12 months, payable in advance;
- one-time admission and onboarding fee: **£7,500**;
- first-year total: **£42,500**, before applicable taxes and separate third-party charges.

3.3 The public standard price may change for future purchasers. Management's internal price strategy does not form part of this Agreement unless expressly inserted into a Membership Schedule.

3.4 The initial Dodo Payments checkout is intended as a one-time purchase of a 12-month membership plus a one-time admission/onboarding product, not a silent auto-renewal.

3.5 Third-party goods and services — including travel, accommodation, event tickets, transport, property, healthcare, education, aviation, professional advice and other externally supplied items — are separate from the membership fee unless the Membership Schedule expressly states otherwise.

3.6 Taxes, duties, levies, payment charges, bank charges and similar amounts are payable as stated on the applicable invoice/checkout and as required by law.

3.7 Where GSM agrees a different billing currency, the Membership Schedule or checkout states the fixed contractual amount. GSM is not required to apply a live retail foreign-exchange rate.

3.8 GSM may refuse or suspend services where an amount is overdue, reversed, disputed or declined, subject to applicable law.

## 4. Cancellation, cooling-off and refunds

4.1 Nothing in this Agreement removes a statutory cancellation or refund right that cannot lawfully be waived.

4.2 Where a statutory cooling-off right applies and the Member asks GSM to begin onboarding or services before that period expires, the Member expressly requests early performance. GSM may deduct or retain the value of services properly supplied before cancellation only to the extent applicable law permits.

4.3 Outside mandatory rights, refunds and the treatment of the admission/onboarding fee are governed by this Agreement, the Membership Schedule, the reason for termination and services already supplied.

4.4 Third-party deposits, tickets, bookings, professional fees and other supplier charges may be subject to the supplier's own cancellation/refund rules and may be non-refundable.

4.5 **Counsel to finalise the UK/EU/other consumer wording and the precise status of the admission/onboarding fee following acceptance and commencement of work.**

## 5. Private service and third-party suppliers

5.1 Montvelle may research, source, coordinate or introduce third-party providers requested by the Member.

5.2 Unless GSM expressly agrees in writing to be the supplier or principal, the relevant third party — not GSM — supplies the goods, professional advice or services and is responsible for its own acts, omissions, licences, professional duties, service terms and outcomes.

5.3 The Member is responsible for deciding whether to instruct or transact with a third party and for due diligence appropriate to the Member's circumstances.

5.4 GSM does not guarantee availability, reservations, upgrades, event access, aviation, school places, visas, immigration outcomes, property transactions, investment opportunities, medical outcomes, professional outcomes, introductions or any other third-party result.

5.5 A supplier's own terms, deposits, cancellation rules, insurance requirements and liability provisions may apply directly to the Member.

## 6. Commissions, referrals and conflicts

6.1 GSM may have preferred commercial arrangements with suppliers and may receive commissions, referral fees, rebates, benefits, preferred rates or other consideration.

6.2 Material arrangements that could reasonably affect a Member's decision will be disclosed where required by law or Montvelle policy.

6.3 Trusted Partner participation does not purchase member identities, confidential-room access or unrestricted prospecting rights.

6.4 **Gary to approve Montvelle's commission/conflict policy before supplier monetisation begins.**

## 7. No regulated or professional advice by Montvelle

7.1 Montvelle is a private membership, community, research, coordination and lifestyle service. GSM is not acting as a law firm, tax adviser, investment adviser, broker-dealer, immigration adviser, medical provider, fiduciary, trustee, insurer or other regulated professional merely by providing membership, information, introductions or coordination.

7.2 General materials, community discussions, peer perspectives, AI/software outputs and operational suggestions are not a substitute for advice from appropriately qualified professionals instructed for the Member's own circumstances.

7.3 Where a matter requires regulated or licensed advice, the Member must obtain that advice from an appropriately qualified professional.

7.4 GSM will not hold investment/client money, act as escrow or execute regulated transactions unless a separately authorised structure is expressly established.

## 8. Member conduct and protection of the community

8.1 The Member and authorised users must act lawfully, respectfully and consistently with the private nature of Montvelle.

8.2 Unless expressly authorised, the following are prohibited: scraping; bulk export; list building; credential sharing; impersonation; doxxing; unauthorised recording or screenshots; unauthorised forwarding; unsolicited commercial prospecting; recruitment poaching; harassment; circumvention of access controls; misuse of confidential information; and use of Montvelle/member data to train an external model or dataset.

8.3 The Member must not represent that GSM, Montvelle or another member endorses an investment, transaction, product, provider or the Member unless expressly authorised in writing.

8.4 GSM may impose reasonable event, guest, venue, communications, information-security and community rules from time to time.

## 9. Confidentiality and introductions

9.1 Information shared in a private Table, Decision Room, member-only area, private gathering, concierge request, family context, ownership context, adviser context or confidential communication must be treated as confidential where a reasonable person would understand it to be private.

9.2 Members may use general learning and ideas from the community but must not identify another person or disclose that person's confidential facts, family circumstances, commercial information or sensitive context without permission.

9.3 Private sessions may not be recorded, transcribed, photographed, screenshotted, forwarded or published without appropriate consent.

9.4 Montvelle introductions are consent-led. GSM may share enough context to assess relevance but should not release private contact details or sensitive case information to another member or Trusted Partner without appropriate consent.

9.5 Confidentiality obligations survive expiry or termination. Disclosure required by law, court order or competent authority is permitted subject to applicable notification restrictions.

9.6 **Counsel to consider injunctive/equitable relief for threatened confidentiality, data and IP breaches.**

## 10. Family and under-18 participants

10.1 A person under 18 may not enter into this Agreement directly with GSM.

10.2 A parent, guardian or other legally authorised adult must approve any under-18 participation and is responsible for ensuring information provided about a child is lawfully shared.

10.3 GSM may apply separate age, supervision, account-access, communications, safeguarding and event rules to next-generation programmes.

10.4 GSM may immediately restrict participation where it reasonably believes safety, welfare, safeguarding or legal compliance is at risk.

## 11. Member information, privacy and AI-assisted tools

11.1 GSM processes personal information in accordance with the Montvelle Privacy Notice and applicable law.

11.2 Member-directory information is private by default. GSM does not sell the member directory or grant third parties unrestricted prospecting access to member identities.

11.3 The Member grants GSM a limited right to host, process, reproduce and transmit information submitted by or for the Member only to the extent reasonably required to provide, secure and administer the service, comply with law and exercise GSM's rights under this Agreement.

11.4 Ownership of the Member's own confidential information remains with the Member or lawful owner.

11.5 GSM may use software and AI-assisted tools to organise information, support research, summarise material, coordinate workflows or surface options. Material membership, safety or similarly significant decisions should include human review where appropriate or legally required.

11.6 Confidential member content should not be used to train a public/general-purpose model without an appropriate legal basis and any permission required by law or Montvelle policy.

## 12. Intellectual property

12.1 GSM or its licensors own the Montvelle name, marks, visual identity, website, software, private frameworks, editorial content and original materials except for material expressly identified as belonging to a Member or third party.

12.2 During active membership, GSM grants the Member a limited, personal, revocable, non-exclusive and non-transferable right to use Montvelle materials for the Member's own private purposes.

12.3 The Member may not reproduce, resell, republish, reverse engineer, commercially exploit, remove proprietary notices from or create confusingly similar products from Montvelle materials except to the extent a non-waivable right permits it.

## 13. Availability and changes

13.1 GSM aims to provide a secure and reliable service but does not guarantee uninterrupted or error-free access.

13.2 GSM may change features, suppliers, programme formats, locations, digital functionality and operating processes where reasonably necessary for security, law, capacity, service quality or product development.

13.3 GSM will not use a routine product update to remove a material paid contractual right during a current term without following this Agreement, the Membership Schedule and mandatory law.

## 14. Compliance, sanctions and prohibited activity

14.1 GSM may refuse, delay, restrict or terminate a membership, request, payment or introduction where GSM reasonably believes continuing could breach sanctions, export controls, anti-bribery law, fraud controls, financial-crime obligations, court orders or other applicable law.

14.2 The Member must not use Montvelle to facilitate illegal payments, bribery, corruption, money laundering, sanctions evasion, fraud, trafficking, prohibited investment activity or other unlawful conduct.

14.3 GSM may request information reasonably required to assess a compliance concern and may make disclosures to competent authorities where legally required.

## 15. Suspension and termination

15.1 GSM may suspend access while investigating a credible security, safety, confidentiality, payment, legal or serious conduct concern.

15.2 GSM may terminate membership for material or repeated breach, confidentiality/no-solicitation breach, harassment, fraud, unlawful activity, material non-payment, reversed payment, serious safety risk, misleading information, sanctions/compliance concerns or conduct that materially threatens the trust, privacy, legal position, reputation or integrity of the community.

15.3 Where appropriate and lawful, significant membership decisions should include human review.

15.4 The financial consequences of termination, including refund or retained fees, are governed by the circumstances, Membership Schedule and mandatory law. **Counsel to distinguish termination for Member breach from GSM termination without Member fault.**

15.5 Provisions intended by nature to survive — including confidentiality, IP, accrued payment obligations, liability, dispute and data-related obligations — continue after membership ends.

## 16. Member responsibility

16.1 The Member remains responsible for personal, family, commercial, investment, tax, legal, immigration, medical, educational, travel and other decisions.

16.2 Information obtained through Montvelle may be incomplete, become outdated or reflect another member's personal experience rather than professional advice.

16.3 The Member is responsible for ensuring that authorised users comply with this Agreement.

## 17. Liability

**Counsel to finalise for enforceability in target jurisdictions.**

17.1 Nothing excludes or limits liability that cannot lawfully be excluded or limited, including fraud/fraudulent misrepresentation and other liabilities required by applicable law.

17.2 Subject to clause 17.1 and to the fullest extent permitted by law, GSM should not be liable for indirect, incidental, consequential, special or punitive loss, or loss of profit, revenue, business opportunity, goodwill, anticipated savings, investment value or data.

17.3 Subject to clause 17.1, GSM should not be liable for the acts, omissions, insolvency, delay, cancellation, negligence or non-performance of an independent third-party supplier merely because Montvelle introduced, recommended or coordinated that supplier, except where applicable law imposes non-excludable responsibility on GSM.

17.4 **Recommended aggregate cap for Gary's approval:** GSM's total aggregate liability for all other losses arising under or in connection with a Membership should not exceed the annual Membership Fee paid or payable to GSM for the relevant 12-month term, subject to any additional statutory carve-outs required by applicable law.

17.5 The liability structure should apply to website, membership, member technology and private-service coordination insofar as legally permissible, with bespoke regulated/principal services governed by their own schedules where required.

## 18. Indemnity

**Counsel to confirm scope and consumer applicability.** Where lawful and appropriate, an entity Member should indemnify GSM against third-party claims arising directly from the entity's unlawful use of Montvelle, infringement caused by material supplied by the entity, or unauthorised acts of its authorised users, excluding losses caused by GSM's own non-excludable fault.

## 19. Events outside reasonable control

Neither party is responsible for delay or failure caused by events outside its reasonable control to the extent permitted by law, including natural disasters, war, terrorism, civil disruption, epidemics/pandemics, government action, transport disruption, widespread communications outages, supplier failures or similar force-majeure events. Mandatory rights remain unaffected.

## 20. Electronic contracting and notices

20.1 This Agreement and Membership Schedule may be accepted electronically and in counterparts.

20.2 Routine membership notices may be sent electronically to the contact details held for the Member.

20.3 Formal legal notices must be sent using the notice details stated in the Membership Schedule / Legal Notice.

## 21. Assignment

21.1 The Member may not assign or transfer this Agreement or membership without GSM's prior written consent.

21.2 GSM may assign this Agreement to an affiliate, successor or purchaser of substantially all relevant business/assets, subject to applicable law and without reducing mandatory Member rights.

## 22. Entire agreement, severability and waiver

22.1 The Agreement, Membership Schedule and expressly incorporated policies form the entire agreement for the paid membership relationship and supersede prior representations about that relationship, subject to liability for fraud and non-waivable rights.

22.2 If a provision is invalid or unenforceable, it should be modified/severed only to the minimum extent necessary and remaining provisions continue in effect.

22.3 Failure to enforce a provision is not a waiver.

## 23. Relationship of the parties

Nothing creates a partnership, joint venture, employment relationship, fiduciary relationship, agency or authority for one party to bind the other unless a separate written document expressly states otherwise.

## 24. Governing law and disputes

**Gary / counsel decision required.** Proposed starting point: Delaware law, without prejudice to mandatory rights that apply because of the Member's residence or other applicable law. Counsel should select Delaware courts or confidential arbitration and draft any consumer/jurisdiction carve-outs needed for international enforceability.

## 25. Language

Translations may be provided for convenience. The English-language Agreement is authoritative to the extent permitted by applicable law.
