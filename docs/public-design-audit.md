# Public design audit — elite concierge pass

## Problems removed

- Townhouse/architecture-led opening that made the brand feel static and property-led.
- Dense feature-card walls on the major public pages.
- Founder-first personal story on the About page.
- Local image references that were not present in the public asset tree.
- Excessive dark overlays and repeated software-style section patterns.
- Narrow page canvas that constrained photography.

## New system

- 92rem editorial canvas with narrower reading widths inside it.
- Full-bleed lifestyle hero photography.
- Asymmetric image-led editorial grids.
- 128–192px desktop section rhythm on major public storytelling sections.
- Divider-led service and membership explanations instead of boxed cards.
- Near-black private-office sections used sparingly for contrast.
- Rounded, quiet membership CTAs rather than loud rectangular action bars.
- Major public routes share one luxury editorial rhythm while retaining distinct copy and imagery.

## Product hierarchy

1. Lifestyle and possibility.
2. Belonging and The Table.
3. Personal service.
4. Family and global life.
5. Private-office intelligence.
