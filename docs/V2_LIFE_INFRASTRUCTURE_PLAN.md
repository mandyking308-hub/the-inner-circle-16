# Project Table V2 — Life Infrastructure Plan

## Product leap

V1 proved the private-network mechanics. V2 expands the product from a club/community into a coordinated operating layer for globally mobile families.

Core proposition:

> Success creates complexity. Nobody hands you the map.
>
> The Table gives members the people. The platform gives them the infrastructure.

The product should help a member move from a question to an executable path across family, enterprise, global mobility, education, trusted providers and daily life — without turning the community into a sales marketplace.

## Competitive mechanics to borrow — not copy

- TIGER 21: permanent peer groups, member benefits through collective purchasing power, strict non-solicitation, family and rising-generation focus.
- Family Office Exchange: relationship manager, trusted advisor directory, peer-recommended providers, research/templates, specialist networks, benchmarking and advisor councils.
- GFOC: approved supplier directory, expert councils, recruitment and family-office support services.
- Quintessentially: one point of contact, request execution, education/property/travel/relocation support, global partner network and member benefits.
- Henley & Partners: an integrated global-mobility journey across residence/citizenship, education, tax, property and concierge, coordinated by a relationship manager.
- Acton Academy: learning by doing, mastery, entrepreneurship, apprenticeships, self-management and public evidence of work.
- Soho House / Arts Club / Home House / The Conduit: image-led editorial storytelling, strong sense of place, distinctive voice, events/programming and member identity.

## New product pillars

### 1. Global Life Desk
A cross-border planning workspace for internationally mobile families.

Modules:
- Country and jurisdiction shortlists
- Residence / citizenship pathways
- Day-count and key-date planner
- Tax and legal questions list
- Companies, trusts and fiduciary structures
- Banking, FX and insurance
- Property and household setup
- Schools, universities and alternative education
- Healthcare and wellbeing
- Immigration documents and family logistics
- Adviser map and unresolved decisions

MVP is coordination and decision support, not legal/tax advice.

### 2. Family Architecture
A visual map of the family enterprise and its professional infrastructure.

Modules:
- Family members and roles
- Companies and ownership
- Trusts / foundations / charities
- Property
- Insurance and protection
- Key advisers
- Succession and governance
- Important dates
- Document placeholders / future secure vault

### 3. Family Learning Studio
A personalised education and capability layer for children and rising generations.

Principle: Skill is not execution.

Learning model:
1. KNOW — acquire core knowledge.
2. APPLY — use the skill in a real task.
3. DELIVER — produce something for a deadline, budget, audience or customer.
4. REVIEW — reflect, improve and evidence mastery.

Execution loop:
Brief → Plan → Build → Test → Deliver → Review.

Curriculum domains:
- Core academic mastery
- Communication, writing, speaking and negotiation
- Money, accounting and financial literacy
- Entrepreneurship and commercial thinking
- AI, data and technology
- Practical independence and life administration
- Global citizenship, history and geopolitics
- Arts, culture and creativity
- Health, resilience and wellbeing
- Philanthropy, service and stewardship

Tracks:
- Supplementary family curriculum
- Hybrid / alternative education plan
- Home education planning
- Rising-generation enterprise preparation

Each learner builds an evidence portfolio of projects, exhibitions, apprenticeships, businesses, presentations, volunteering and real work.

### 4. Rising Generation & Alumni Network
A protected bridge from Next Gen into adult professional life.

Features:
- 16+/18+ alumni community
- Mentors and office hours
- Founder shadowing
- Apprenticeships and internships
- Project briefs from members and trusted partners
- Skills passport / evidence portfolio
- Alumni events and peer circles
- Opportunities board
- Give-back expectation: alumni later mentor younger cohorts

### 5. Trusted Partner Network
A curated provider ecosystem with a strict firewall from the peer community.

Partner categories:
- Legal / tax
- Trust and fiduciary
- Immigration / residence / citizenship
- Banking / FX
- Insurance
- Property
- Education
- Health / wellbeing
- Security / privacy / reputation
- Technology / cyber
- Recruitment / household staff
- Travel / concierge
- Philanthropy / impact
- Art / collections
- Clubs / hospitality / access

Trust statuses:
- Member Recommended — used and recommended by a member
- Vetted Partner — due diligence and references completed
- Strategic Partner — deeper service/benefit relationship

Partners never buy member contact data or cold access.

### 6. Partner value proposition
Why a high-quality firm would want to work with us:

- Positioning with a curated family-enterprise community
- Inclusion in a trusted directory after screening
- Qualified, consent-led briefs from members who actually need the service
- Thought-leadership opportunities and expert sessions
- Ability to contribute member benefits or preferential terms
- Aggregated, anonymised insight into what globally mobile families are struggling with
- Peer community with other non-competing trusted advisers
- Referral opportunities among advisers
- Research participation and co-created practical guides
- A partner mark for approved firms, subject to brand rules
- Aggregate engagement analytics without exposing individual member data

Commercial models to test later:
- annual partner membership
- sponsored research / programming
- paid expert workshops
- member benefit partnerships
- request / RFP success fee only where legally and ethically appropriate

Never charge purely for access to member identities.

### 7. Concierge & Execution Desk
The community should help members execute, not just discuss.

Request categories:
- Travel
- Events and cultural access
- Private clubs / reciprocal access guidance
- Restaurants / hospitality
- Relocation
- Education
- Property
- Household staff
- Gifting / occasions
- Health and wellbeing
- Security / privacy
- Family administration

Request workflow:
Request → Clarify → Match → Consent → Execute → Confirm → Rate / recommend.

### 8. Reciprocal / Places Network
Over time, build reciprocal relationships with clubs, hotels, workspaces and trusted venues around the world.

MVP:
- curated places directory
- member benefit / reciprocal status
- city guides
- request access via concierge

Do not promise club membership or access that has not been formally agreed.

### 9. Intelligence & Playbooks
Turn recurring problems into institutional knowledge.

Playbook examples:
- Moving country with children
- Choosing a jurisdiction
- Building a family-office-lite structure
- Selecting trustees
- Setting up a family investment committee
- Choosing schools internationally
- Creating a family curriculum
- Preparing a child for business ownership
- Hiring advisers
- Coordinating tax/legal/immigration advice
- Preparing for incapacity/death
- Philanthropy structure
- Cyber/privacy for a family

### 10. Visual personality
The current site is too text-led.

Design evolution:
- image-led editorial sections
- strong full-bleed visual moments
- original monochrome / bronze line illustrations rather than generic SaaS graphics
- maps, diagrams and illustrated objects: a table, globe, passport, ledger, school desk, doorway, family tree, city skyline, compass
- large magazine-style headlines
- alternating warm ivory and ink sections
- candid human-scale storytelling rather than generic luxury language
- pages should feel like a private magazine + family office + members club

## Safety and trust constraints

- No member-data sale.
- No supplier cold outreach through the platform.
- Consent before introductions.
- Clear conflicts disclosure.
- Trusted Partner is a curated status, not a guarantee.
- Legal/tax/immigration/education content is informational and routes to qualified professionals.
- Next Gen and alumni permissions remain age-appropriate and guardian-controlled where required.
- Real family architecture data must wait for production authentication, private repository, database security and appropriate encryption.
