# Public design reference — locked 22 Aug 2026

## North stars

- Quintessentially: lifestyle first, real editorial photography, global cultural relevance, services woven into the world rather than presented like software.
- Knightsbridge Circle: selective membership, confidence, personal-office positioning, proactive service and a sense of one trusted relationship around the member.

## Project Table interpretation

Project Table must not copy either brand. It combines their visual confidence with Project Table's own deeper proposition:

1. The world gets them through the door.
2. The people make them stay.
3. The private operating system makes the membership indispensable.

## Non-negotiables

- Real editorial photography; no obvious AI imagery.
- One strong thought per viewport.
- Wide cinematic imagery and generous negative space.
- No feature-card walls on major public pages.
- Warm off-white, ink black and quiet mineral tones; oxblood/bronze only as restrained accents.
- Membership, people, places, family, access and service before technology.
- Private-office technology appears later and is described as quiet infrastructure, never the personality of the brand.
- No founder's personal family story used as public marketing copy.
