# Montvelle — Gary legal review pack

**Date:** 23 August 2026  
**Status:** Counsel-review draft. Not final legal advice and not for member signature until Gary/counsel approves it.  
**Operating entity:** **Global Solutions Management LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("GSM").  
**Brand:** **Montvelle**.  
**Commercial model:** Global private membership combining peer community, gatherings, introductions, concierge/private-office coordination, family learning and digital member tools.

## 1. Commercial decision now taken

Montvelle will publish its current standard price on the public Membership page and allow a prospective member to continue to a secure Dodo Payments hosted checkout.

**2026 founding price**
- Annual membership: **£35,000**, for a 12-month term, paid in advance.
- One-time admission & onboarding fee: **£7,500**.
- First-year total before applicable taxes and separately purchased third-party goods/services: **£42,500**.

**Growth pricing strategy:** management's current commercial intention is to increase the headline annual price for **new memberships** by £10,000 for each new pricing year as Montvelle grows (illustratively £35k → £45k → £55k → £65k). This is an internal pricing strategy, not a contractual promise. Existing/renewing members should pay the fee stated in their renewal invitation / Membership Schedule. Gary should approve the notice period and whether any members are ever grandfathered.

For launch, the Dodo annual-membership product should be configured as a **one-time 12-month purchase rather than an auto-renewing subscription**. This makes each renewal a fresh offer at the then-current price and avoids a silent renewal while the international consumer position is being finalised.

## 2. What comparable membership businesses actually do

The market benchmark supports a strong but conventional structure rather than unusually aggressive terms.

### TIGER 21
- Publishes membership dues and a one-time initiation fee.
- 2026 UK rate: £25,500 dues + £4,000 initiation fee.
- Membership activates only after full initiation fee, full dues and a signed Membership Agreement are received.
- Renewal pricing is the price in force at the beginning of the new term.
- Pricing is reviewed/increased annually under a published policy.
- Its separate platform/app terms include arbitration, broad warranty exclusions, termination rights, indemnity and a liability cap; its Membership Agreement is the controlling membership contract.

**Useful Montvelle lesson:** public price is compatible with a premium peer network; payment should not itself be the only activation condition; the Membership Agreement should prevail; future renewal pricing can be the then-current fee.

### Quintessentially
- Annual membership fees are paid in advance; joining fees are charged on acceptance.
- Fees for future years may change and are notified before renewal.
- Global terms recognise a 14-day cancellation period where applicable and allow a deduction for services already performed where the member requested immediate performance.
- Supplier contracts are generally between member and supplier.
- Quintessentially reserves the right to receive supplier commissions.
- Its global liability wording excludes categories of business/economic loss and caps aggregate liability for other losses at the annual Membership Fee.
- It has strong non-solicitation, payment, suspension and termination rights.

**Useful Montvelle lesson:** an annual-fee liability cap is a serious market precedent; supplier independence, commission disclosure, cooling-off mechanics and immediate-service consent should be explicit.

### Knightsbridge Circle
- Uses a 12-month membership contract and publishes ultra-premium pricing publicly.
- Annual fee is paid in advance; its published application terms contemplate bank transfer/invoice and, in its model, annual renewal unless proper notice is given.
- It expressly separates third-party suppliers from the membership company and makes the member responsible for supplier charges.
- It provides a 14-day consumer cancellation right in its UK/EMEA form, with deduction for services/expenses already incurred if work begins.
- It can suspend for non-payment and decline requests outside its legal/ethical/service remit.
- Supplier commissions are expressly disclosed in its commercial model.

**Useful Montvelle lesson:** high-value public pricing and strong supplier/liability protections are normal, but consumer rights cannot simply be contracted away.

## 3. Recommended Montvelle legal architecture

The paid relationship should be governed in this order:
1. Membership Schedule / bespoke service schedule.
2. Montvelle Membership Agreement.
3. Incorporated Confidentiality, Privacy and conduct policies.
4. Public Website Terms.

Payment-page copy should never be relied upon as the whole contract.

## 4. Decisions Gary should approve today

### A. GSM as contracting entity
Confirm that GSM is the sole contracting entity for global Montvelle membership unless a future written schedule says otherwise. Insert GSM's exact Delaware file number, registered/business address, formal notices email and billing contact.

### B. When the contract begins
Recommended: payment may be taken first, but membership **activates only after** GSM acceptance, applicable identity/sanctions/fraud/safety checks, acceptance of the Membership Agreement/Schedule and cleared funds. If GSM declines an unactivated paid membership, the membership/admission fees should be returned.

Gary should confirm whether the payment itself forms a conditional contract, an offer by the purchaser, or another structure in the target jurisdictions.

### C. Consumer / cooling-off treatment
Do not assume a wealthy individual is automatically contracting as a business. Confirm UK/EU and other target-country rules.

Recommended checkout acknowledgement: the purchaser expressly asks GSM to begin onboarding/service preparation once accepted and acknowledges that, where law permits, the value of services already supplied can be deducted from a cancellation refund.

Gary should approve final words before live mode is enabled.

### D. Renewal and price increases
Recommended launch structure:
- 12-month fixed term;
- no automatic Dodo renewal at launch;
- renewal is a fresh invitation at GSM's then-current price;
- renewal price notified sufficiently in advance;
- GSM has no obligation to grandfather a previous price unless it expressly agrees to do so.

This supports the planned £10,000 annual new-member price step-up without creating a promise about future renewals.

### E. Liability cap
Recommended starting point for Gary: subject to all non-excludable liabilities, aggregate GSM liability for paid membership should be capped at the **annual Membership Fee paid/payable for the relevant 12-month term**, with appropriate exclusions for indirect/consequential/special loss, profit/revenue/opportunity/goodwill/data and independent supplier acts/omissions to the fullest extent enforceable.

This broadly follows the Quintessentially market structure and is more defensible for the paid relationship than an unusually low nominal cap.

### F. Third-party suppliers
Montvelle may research, source, coordinate and introduce suppliers but, unless GSM expressly contracts as principal, the supplier remains responsible for its goods, professional advice, licences, terms and performance. The member decides whether to contract with the supplier and is responsible for supplier charges and relevant due diligence.

No guarantees of reservations, upgrades, aviation, schools, visas, investments, property transactions, medical outcomes or professional outcomes.

### G. Commissions and conflicts
Decide whether GSM may receive commission, referral fees, rebates, preferred rates or non-cash benefits from hotels, travel providers, advisers and other suppliers. Market competitors expressly reserve this right. If Montvelle does it, use a transparent conflict/disclosure standard rather than pretending all recommendations are commission-free.

### H. Suspension / termination
GSM should be able to refuse, suspend or terminate for:
- non-payment, reversal or chargeback;
- serious/repeated breach;
- confidentiality or solicitation breach;
- harassment or safety risk;
- false/misleading information;
- fraud or misuse;
- sanctions, bribery, financial crime or legal restrictions;
- serious reputational/operational risk where reasonably determined;
- abuse of suppliers, staff or other members.

Gary should approve refund consequences for termination with cause versus GSM ending a membership without member fault.

### I. Regulated services boundary
GSM/Montvelle is not a law firm, investment adviser, broker-dealer, tax adviser, immigration adviser, healthcare provider, fiduciary, trustee or escrow/payment custodian merely because it provides membership, research, introductions or coordination. Regulated matters must be independently instructed to qualified professionals.

### J. Confidentiality / non-solicitation
Make the current Montvelle Confidentiality & No Solicitation standard contractually binding. Cover no recording, screenshots, scraping, bulk export, lead harvesting, poaching, doxxing, unauthorised forwarding and use of member content to train external models. Obligations should survive termination.

### K. Family / under-18s
Under-18s must not contract directly with GSM. Parent/guardian authority, safeguarding, communications, event supervision and data controls should be documented separately where children participate.

### L. Privacy / international data
Confirm GSM as controller/responsible business for core Montvelle processing. Review UK GDPR Article 27 / EU GDPR representative requirements based on actual targeting and establishment, international transfer mechanisms, DPAs/subprocessors, retention, AI-assisted tooling and the privacy contact details to publish.

### M. Governing law / dispute resolution
Current website starting point is Delaware law subject to mandatory local rights. Gary should choose between Delaware courts and confidential arbitration for the paid Membership Agreement and confirm the consumer carve-outs needed internationally.

### N. Tax / invoicing
Finance/tax counsel to confirm US sales-tax exposure, UK/EU VAT place-of-supply rules, GST/VAT elsewhere, invoice wording, tax IDs and whether regional fixed prices should be quoted rather than FX-converted at checkout.

## 5. Dodo Payments structure

Montvelle's website creates the checkout session on the **server** and sends the purchaser to Dodo's hosted checkout. Raw payment credentials are not collected by Montvelle.

Required production secrets/configuration:
- `DODO_PAYMENTS_API_KEY`
- `DODO_PAYMENTS_ENVIRONMENT=live_mode`
- `DODO_MONTVELLE_ANNUAL_PRODUCT_ID`
- `DODO_MONTVELLE_JOINING_PRODUCT_ID`
- `MONTVELLE_PUBLIC_URL=https://montvelle.com`

Until those are configured, the code defaults to Dodo test mode / will not create a real charge.

The Dodo annual product should initially be created as a **one-time £35,000 product representing 12 months of membership** and the joining fee as a **one-time £7,500 product**. Both are placed into one hosted Checkout Session. Next year's renewal can use a new product/price at the then-current Montvelle rate.

A production webhook should ultimately confirm payment and protect against relying on browser return URLs. Activation should occur only after verified payment status plus the legal/admission conditions.

## 7. Key instruction for counsel

The objective is **maximum lawful protection for GSM, not maximum aggressive wording**. Provisions that ignore mandatory consumer/privacy law can be less protective because they may be unenforceable. Montvelle should use the strongest commercially reasonable structure that remains enforceable across the countries in which it actually sells membership.
