# Montvelle — legal review pack for Gary

**Status:** Counsel review draft. Not final legal advice and not for member signature until approved.

**Operating entity:** Global Solutions Management LLC, a Delaware limited liability company ("GSM").

**Brand:** Montvelle.

**Commercial model:** Private, invitation-only global membership combining peer community, gatherings, introductions, concierge/private-office coordination, family learning and digital member tools.

## 1. What the current website already does well

The current public legal architecture already states that Montvelle is operated by GSM; separates Montvelle from independently instructed regulated professionals; disclaims guarantees of investment, immigration, medical, school, transaction and other outcomes; separates third-party suppliers from Montvelle; protects confidentiality and no-solicitation; reserves suspension/termination rights; addresses force majeure; preserves mandatory local rights; and contains a privacy-by-default notice covering international transfers, family/under-18 information, security, retention, member-directory restrictions and consent-led introductions.

The public website terms deliberately defer core paid-membership rights to a separate written Membership Agreement / order form. That is the correct architecture for a high-value global membership, but it means the Membership Agreement must now be completed before paid launch.

## 2. Decisions Gary should sign off today

### A. Contracting entity and disclosures
- Confirm GSM is the sole contracting entity for Montvelle membership globally.
- Insert GSM's exact legal name, Delaware file/registration number if desired, registered/business address, formal legal-notices email and billing contact.
- Confirm whether any local affiliate will ever contract, employ staff, invoice or deliver regulated services. If not, keep all paid membership contracting in GSM.

### B. Governing law and disputes
- Current website terms use Delaware law subject to mandatory local rights.
- Decide the paid Membership Agreement dispute model: Delaware state/federal courts, confidential arbitration, or another forum.
- For an UHNW global membership, confidential arbitration may be commercially attractive, but enforceability, consumer treatment and cost should be reviewed by counsel.
- Preserve mandatory rights that cannot lawfully be waived in the member's home jurisdiction.

### C. Consumer vs business contracting
- Do not assume an HNW individual is automatically a business customer.
- Confirm UK/EU/other distance-selling and cooling-off rules for individuals purchasing membership online.
- If service begins immediately, add the legally required express request/acknowledgement where applicable rather than relying on a blanket 'non-refundable' statement.

### D. Fees, renewals and payment
Proposed structure for review:
- annual membership paid in full in advance;
- one-time admission/onboarding fee;
- no silent auto-renewal at launch: renewal is by fresh invitation/invoice each year;
- third-party goods/services billed separately;
- taxes, duties, bank charges and FX treatment stated clearly;
- refunds governed by the agreement and mandatory law;
- chargeback/non-payment rights and service suspension covered;
- membership starts only after agreement acceptance, required checks and cleared funds.

### E. Liability protection
The website currently defers paid-membership liability to the Membership Agreement. Gary should approve:
- aggregate liability cap;
- exclusion of indirect, consequential, special and loss-of-profit/opportunity damages to the fullest extent permitted;
- no liability for independent third-party suppliers beyond non-excludable law;
- no guarantee of access, reservations, introductions, investment/business outcomes, school places, visas, medical outcomes or professional advice;
- member responsibility for independent due diligence and professional instructions;
- carve-outs for fraud and other liabilities that cannot lawfully be excluded.

### F. Third-party suppliers, commissions and conflicts
- Decide whether GSM/Montvelle may receive commissions, referral fees, rebates, preferred rates or non-cash benefits from suppliers.
- If yes, state the disclosure standard and conflict process clearly.
- Avoid describing suppliers as 'at cost' unless this is operationally true in every applicable case.
- Trusted Partner participation must never buy access to member data or confidential rooms.

### G. Regulated services boundary
Montvelle should remain a membership, coordination, community and lifestyle platform unless separately licensed activity is intentionally introduced.
- No legal, tax, investment, securities, immigration, medical, fiduciary or regulated financial advice by Montvelle itself.
- Qualified professionals are independently instructed and responsible for their own advice/licensing.
- No holding client money for investments or transactions unless a properly regulated payment/escrow model is separately established.

### H. Sanctions, anti-bribery and financial crime
Add a contractual right to refuse, suspend or terminate where membership or a request could breach sanctions, export controls, anti-bribery law, fraud controls or other financial-crime obligations.
- Decide the level of identity/background screening at admission.
- Decide whether source-of-funds/source-of-wealth checks are required for particular services even if not required for ordinary membership.

### I. Confidentiality and member conduct
Make the existing Confidentiality & No Solicitation standard contractually binding.
- no recording, scraping, bulk export, doxxing, unsolicited prospecting or unauthorised forwarding;
- consent before introductions/contact data release;
- injunctive/equitable relief wording for threatened confidentiality/IP breaches if counsel approves;
- clear survival of confidentiality obligations after membership ends.

### J. Children / next generation
- Under-18s should not contract directly with GSM.
- Parent/guardian authority and consent should be documented.
- Set age-appropriate account, event, supervision, communications and data boundaries.
- Review safeguarding obligations in each programme/jurisdiction in which Montvelle directly organises activity involving minors.

### K. Privacy and global data
- Confirm GSM as controller/responsible business for core Montvelle processing.
- Add the actual privacy contact address/email.
- Review whether GSM requires a UK GDPR Article 27 representative and/or EU GDPR representative based on actual establishment and targeting.
- Confirm international-transfer mechanism(s), vendor DPAs, subprocessors and retention schedule.
- Keep member-directory data private by default and prohibit sale/scraping.
- Confirm the treatment of AI-assisted operational tools and that confidential member data is not used to train public/general models without an appropriate legal basis and clear permission.

### L. Tax and invoicing
Finance/tax advisers should confirm:
- US sales-tax exposure where relevant;
- UK/EU VAT place-of-supply rules;
- GST/VAT obligations in other target markets;
- invoice wording and tax IDs;
- whether regional fixed prices or a single base-currency price is preferable.

### M. Boilerplate still required in paid agreement
- entire agreement and order of precedence;
- assignment (GSM can assign to affiliate/successor; member cannot transfer without consent);
- notices/e-signatures;
- severability;
- waiver;
- survival;
- amendments;
- relationship of parties / no agency / no fiduciary relationship;
- force majeure;
- language/translation precedence;
- counterparts/electronic acceptance.

## 3. Recommended pricing position for counsel/finance review

Montvelle sits commercially between UHNW peer networks and ultra-premium concierge/private-office services. A credible initial standard position is:

**Montvelle annual membership: £35,000 per membership per year, paid annually in advance.**

**One-time admission & onboarding fee: £7,500.**

The accepted-member page should allow GSM to quote an equivalent fixed amount in another billing currency in the invitation/order form rather than relying on a live FX conversion.

For unusually high service intensity, a separate bespoke Private Office arrangement can be priced independently rather than weakening the standard membership economics.

Do not publish the standard fee prominently on the public marketing site at launch. The public journey should remain application → fit conversation → approval → private acceptance page → signed agreement → annual payment.

## 4. Competitor pattern informing that recommendation

- TIGER 21 publicly lists UK dues of £25,500 plus a £4,000 initiation fee for 2026, and $51,500 plus $5,000 for its Family Office Groups.
- Knightsbridge Circle publicly lists membership from $55,000/year plus a $25,000 joining fee, and a 1:1 tier at $210,000/year plus a $50,000 joining fee.
- Quintessentially does not publish a fixed price and says pricing is personalised after consultation.
- Campden Wealth has historically published lower annual family-network pricing in membership materials.
- Family Office Exchange generally routes prospects through membership inquiry rather than displaying a simple public checkout price.

Montvelle therefore should not look cheap, but it also should not price itself as a dedicated 1:1 concierge service until staffing and service ratios genuinely support that promise.

## 5. Payment-page architecture

The private acceptance/payment page should be unlinked from public navigation and marked `noindex,nofollow`. It should show only the fee actually offered to that accepted member and should not rely on secrecy of the URL as the security control.

Recommended flow:
1. application approved;
2. member receives unique secure acceptance link;
3. final Membership Agreement and key commercial terms displayed/downloadable;
4. member confirms legal name, billing details and agreement acceptance;
5. annual fee + admission fee shown clearly, with taxes if applicable;
6. payment by bank transfer/wire as the default high-value route, with secure card/ACH option where commercially sensible;
7. payment confirmation recorded;
8. membership activated only after required checks, signed terms and cleared funds.

A payment processor or bank-transfer workflow must be connected before this page becomes a live checkout. Do not collect raw card or bank credentials in Montvelle's own application code.
