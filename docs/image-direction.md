# Image direction

Use images that feel observed rather than staged: a real traveller looking out at a destination, a family moving through a place together, an intimate private table, service moments, architecture with life around it, culture and a quiet private office.

Avoid stock-boardroom behaviour, posed handshakes, repeated private jets, champagne cliches, model-perfect eye contact and dark wealth-signalling imagery.

The current public pass uses external Unsplash editorial photography so the public pages remain real-image-led without spending Lovable generation credits.
